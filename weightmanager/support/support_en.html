Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What can I do with Daily Weight Recorder?

You can record your weight, body fat percentage, calorie intake, calories burned, and other related information, and review your daily changes in graphs. The app also supports target weight and target body fat settings, as well as meal and workout template management.

Q. Where is my recorded data stored?

In principle, recorded data is stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, your data may be stored and synchronized through your iCloud environment using Apple iCloud / CloudKit or related services.

Q. Is iCloud sync required?

No. iCloud sync is optional. You can choose whether to use iCloud sync in the app settings.

Q. Can I delete my recorded data?

Yes. You can delete recorded data and settings using app features or device operations. If iCloud sync is enabled, it may take some time for the deletion to be reflected in the synchronized data.

Q. Does the app display ads?

Yes. This app may display ads using Google AdMob. Ad content, ad availability, delivery frequency, personalization, and other ad behavior may depend on Google or its partners.

Q. Does the app store Face ID or Touch ID information?

No. Authentication using Face ID, Touch ID, or a device passcode is performed through mechanisms provided by Apple. Biometric information itself is not stored by this app or on the developer's servers.

Q. Is this app a medical app?

No. Daily Weight Recorder is a general recording and visualization tool intended to support health management. It does not provide medical practice, diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice.

Please consult a physician or other qualified professional before making important health-related decisions.
